# Alundra

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains the options you need to configure and export a
config file. The only Alundra option is "Vanilla Crests", which keeps every crest in its original location; the rest
uses the same settings as the randomizer's default preset.

## What does randomization do to this game?

The items found in chests are shuffled between all the worlds of the multiworld. The seven crests must be collected
and placed in the Lake Shrine to reach the final boss, and the game is won by defeating Melzas.

The game starts in Jess's house with a Dagger, Cloth Armor and Short Boots. Equipment with several tiers is not
progressive: each weapon, armor and boots is its own item.

## Which locations are checked?

258 chests, including the ones given by bosses and mini-bosses. Shops, Merrick's falcon trades, Lurvy and Naomi's
items, the casino second rewards and a few scripted rewards keep their vanilla item: they are not locations.

## What happens when I open a chest holding an item of another world?

The chest shows an "Archipelago item" and gives nothing. The client tells the server, then shows a message such as
"Sent Hunter's Bow to Player 2".

## What happens when I receive an item?

The game gives you the item as if you had opened a chest and shows "Received Fire Wand from Player 2" with the item
sound. The pose where Alundra holds the item overhead is not played.

## Known limitations

* The pose of Alundra holding an item is not played for items received from other players.
* Progressive equipment is not available.
* Death link, hints and the other randomizer options are not available yet.
