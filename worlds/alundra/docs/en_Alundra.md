# Alundra

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Items found in chests, shops, quest rewards and boss drops are shuffled between all the worlds in the multiworld.
The seven crests must be collected and placed in the Lake Shrine to reach the final boss.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Melzas.

## Which items and locations are shuffled?

Every item source of the Alundra Item Randomizer (291 locations), including shops and the Merrick falcon trades.
